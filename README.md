# Philipp J. Schmidt

I am a doctoral researcher at [Porsche AG](https://www.porsche.com) and a PhD student at the [Technical University of Munich (TUM)](https://www.tum.de).

My research focuses on applying deep learning to complex real-world problems, particularly in two areas.
- Root Cause Analysis — Identifying and diagnosing faults in distributed systems using logs, traces and other observability data, primarily through my research at [Porsche](https://www.porsche.com).
- Acoustic Imaging — Exploring deep learning approaches to acoustic source localisation and imaging, in collaboration with [Meta Reality Labs Research](https://research.facebook.com).

You can find my publications and patents on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=JxMqhLwAAAAJ), and either access my CV on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/philipp-schmidt-industry/) or on my [personal website](https://philipp-schmidt.online).
